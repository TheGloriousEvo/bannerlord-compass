# Bannerlord Compass

Bannerlord Compass adds a compass to the bottom of the screen in Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord multiplayer. Call out a bearing like **075°** so your teammates know which way to look.

## What it shows

- A fixed pointer and a scrolling 120-degree scale with a three-digit bearing.
- Your camera's direction during free look, on horseback, and in first or third person.
- Shared map bearings. North follows the scene’s +Y axis, and east follows +X.
- A passive HUD that hides while you are spectating, in a menu, or viewing the scoreboard.

## Settings

Open **Options > Gameplay > Compass**, or **Escape > Options > Gameplay > Compass** during a match. You can turn the compass on or off, change its width, text size, bottom offset and background opacity, or toggle cardinal directions. The preview updates as you edit. Select **Done** to save your choices.

## Multiplayer compatibility

Bannerlord Compass uses Bannerlord’s optional multiplayer module support. Install it on your client. The server must allow optional modules. Official matchmaking compatibility is unverified.
